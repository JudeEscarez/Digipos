How Lead Radar works

A short guide for sales reps, managers and anyone watching the demo.

What Lead Radar does

  • Finds businesses worth visiting across the UAE and ranks them, so reps start with the most promising ones.
  • Explains every ranking in plain words, so nobody has to trust a number blindly.
  • Learns from visits. When a rep reports back, the list updates for everyone.
  • Watches existing customers and flags those whose card volume is falling, so they can be called before they leave.

Quick start

  1. On the Leads tab, choose an Emirate (for example Dubai).
  2. Start at the top of Leads to visit. The list is sorted by score, highest first.
  3. Click a business. Lead details shows why it ranks there and a ready-made WhatsApp message.
  4. Send the message or visit in person.
  5. Afterwards, answer “What happened at the visit?”. The list updates straight away.

How a score is worked out

Every business gets up to 100 points from five things. The detail panel shows each one as a bar.

PartMaxWhat earns points
Newly opened30Opened recently. New businesses are the most likely to still need a terminal. Under 3 months scores full points.
Business size25More Google reviews and a higher price level suggest more customers paying. For brand-new businesses it's estimated from the type and area.
Fits card payments20Cafés and restaurants score highest, then shops, salons and clinics. Small chains lose 3 points, and developer-run sites lose 5, because a head office may decide.
Busy area15Busy commercial areas score higher. This goes down when reps find terminals nearby (see “How it learns”).
Easy to contact106 points for a listed phone number and 4 for a website.

A few extra rules:

  • Reviews that say the shop is cash only: +5.
  • Reviews that show cards are already accepted: −25.
  • A restricted business type (for example tobacco or money exchange) scores 0 and shouldn't be visited.
The point values are starting assumptions for the demo. They can be changed in one place once real results come in.

AI review check. Before the demo, an AI model searches the web for each business (review sites, delivery apps, its own website) and reads what it finds. It answers four questions: is it cash only, does it already take cards, what type of business is it, and could it be a restricted category. Lead details shows what it found and the exact words it relied on, so anyone can check it against the real review.

  • If the reviews don't clearly say, the answer is “unclear” and the score doesn't change.
  • The quoted words must appear on a page the AI actually opened, and that page must name the business. If not, the answer is thrown out.
  • The AI never marks a business as restricted. It only adds a Check with bank tag for a person to confirm.
  • It runs once ahead of time and the answers are saved, so the demo never depends on the AI or the internet.

Logging a visit

ButtonWhat happens
InterestedThe business stays in the list, tagged “Interested”, so someone follows up.
Not nowThe business stays in the list, tagged “Not now”, to try again later.
Already has a terminalThe business moves to the bottom. Other leads in the same area drop 6 points (up to 15 in total).
Signed upThe business moves to the bottom and is counted in “Signed up” at the top.

Pressed the wrong button? Use ↶ Undo last action in the detail panel. It puts every score back exactly as it was.

How it learns

If one business in an area already has a card terminal, its neighbours probably have been approached too. So the system quietly lowers the priority of that area and moves reps' attention elsewhere.

1 · A rep reportsArt of Teeth Dental Clinic in Al Barsha already has a terminal.
2 · The area is updatedIn Areas, Al Barsha's “Terminals found” goes from 0 to 1.
3 · Neighbours move downThe other Al Barsha clinics lose 6 points each and slide down the list (for example 63 → 57).

You can see all three on screen at once: the rows flash and show ▼ 6, the Areas row turns red, and Recent visits records what happened.

Common questions

Why does almost every lead show 8 / 30 for “Newly opened”?
Opening dates haven't been added to the spreadsheet yet, so every established business gets the same default. Once the dates are filled in, newer businesses will rise to the top.
Why do “Example” rows score so high?
They're set up as brand-new openings, which earn full points for newness. That's the point of the new-openings lane. They only appear when Type is set to New openings or All.
Why did a business move down when I didn't touch it?
A rep found a terminal at a business in the same area. Check Recent visits to see which one.
Can I put everything back the way it was?
Use Undo last action for one step, or Reset demo in the bottom bar to clear all logged visits.
Does it need the internet?
The live version reads from the online database. There's also an offline copy that runs on a laptop with no connection. The bottom bar shows which one you're using.
Who can log visits?
Anyone with the link, unless a demo passcode is switched on. If it is, you'll be asked for the code once per browser.
